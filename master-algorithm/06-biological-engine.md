- **Papers:** NEAT DOI above; Novelty DOI; MAP-Elites arXiv:1504.04909
- **Grows:** network genotype complexity + **QD archive** of elites

### C. Growing Neural Cellular Automata

### D. DreamCoder / “How to Grow a Mind”

## Three falsifiable laptop tests

1. **T1 — NEAT complexification (Evolutionary archive)**  
   Run `neat-python` on XOR ≥50 generations. **Pass if** mean `#nodes+#connections` rises with fitness, and a novelty-search variant keeps higher behavioral diversity at matched fitness than fitness-only. *Falsifies:* “NEAT is just another weight trainer.”

2. **T2 — NCA regeneration (morphogenesis)**  
   Train Distill Growing NCA to a target from a seed; freeze weights; ablate a spatial region; continue CA steps. **Pass if** target recovers below an MSE threshold while a static decoder baseline does not. *Falsifies:* “Growth is only training-time memorization.”

3. **T3 — NDP or pymdp structure growth**  
   (A) NDP on CartPole/XOR: developmental edge count must increase before policy succeeds; or (B) pymdp concept/slot task: hidden-state count expands after a novel observation class then performance rises. **Pass if** structure metric tracks necessity, not wall-clock epochs alone. *Falsifies:* “Nothing here develops beyond gradient fitting.”
